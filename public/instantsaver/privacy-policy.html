Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how InstantSaver, developed by Himanshu Choudhary in Haryana, India, handles information when you use our mobile app, website and support services. InstantSaver helps you preview and save supported Instagram and Facebook media.

Contact: bohimanshu@gmail.com.

Information we handleService providersYour choicesRequest deletion

1. Information we handle and why

  • Media links and content. The app processes links you paste or open, associated post information and media URLs to find, preview and download supported content. Requests are sent to the relevant social platform and media hosts, which receive network information such as your IP address. Downloaded files are saved to your device’s gallery; temporary files may be used during downloading.
  • Clipboard. The app can check your clipboard when its home screen becomes active and when you tap Paste. A supported social link can be used to begin retrieving a media preview.
  • Social login and session cookies. Login takes place in an embedded Instagram or Facebook browser. These platforms handle the credentials you enter. The app uses persisted session cookies to recognize your login and request content available to your account. Treat these cookies as sensitive login information.
  • Social profile information. When you log in, the app can retrieve your platform user ID, username, full name and profile picture URL. Profile synchronization can send these details, your platform/profile URL, a RevenueCat customer identifier and a push notification token to our Supabase database to maintain app profiles and notification delivery.
  • Support messages. If you use Contact Us, your name, email address and message are sent to our support database so we can handle your request. Do not send passwords or session cookies.
  • Usage, diagnostics and security. Integrated services can process app interactions, device and app identifiers, operating-system information, crash reports, performance information and network metadata to understand usage, troubleshoot failures and protect the app from abuse.
  • Notifications. Firebase Cloud Messaging generates a device notification token. The app can associate this token with your synced profile. Notification delivery and display depend on your device permissions and settings.
  • Advertising. Unity LevelPlay and its mediation partners can process device identifiers, IP address, device information and ad interaction information to deliver and measure advertising and prevent fraud. Personalized advertising preferences are available in Settings. Turning personalization off does not necessarily stop advertising or the processing needed to deliver and measure it.
  • Purchase information. Where purchase features are enabled, RevenueCat and the relevant app store process customer identifiers, transaction information and subscription status to provide and restore access. Payment details are handled by the app store under its own policies.

2. Service providers and external platforms

Information is processed by services used to operate the app, including:

  • Google Firebase: Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Cloud Messaging and App Check for usage measurement, diagnostics, notifications and app integrity. See Firebase privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: app configuration, synchronized profiles and support messages. See Supabase’s Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: purchase and entitlement management where enabled. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.
  • Unity LevelPlay and integrated mediation partners: advertising delivery and measurement. See Unity’s Privacy Policy.
  • Instagram and Facebook: social login, profile information and media retrieval are subject to Instagram’s Privacy Policy and Facebook’s Privacy Policy.

These services may process data outside your country. Their processing and retention practices are described in their respective policies. We may also disclose information when required by law, to investigate abuse or to protect users and the service.

3. Permissions and your choices

  • Social login: you can log out of Instagram or Facebook in the app. Logging out is separate from requesting deletion of a synced server profile.
  • Personalization: change the personalized advertising preference in Settings. Your choice is stored on your device and passed to the advertising SDK.
  • Notifications: disable notifications through your operating system’s app settings.
  • Saved media and local data: delete downloaded files in your gallery. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local app data according to your operating system’s behavior; downloaded gallery files and server-side records may remain.
  • Media access: the app uses device storage or gallery access as needed to save files. Permission requirements depend on your device and operating-system version.

4. Access, correction and deletion requests

To request access to, correction of or deletion of personal data held by InstantSaver, email bohimanshu@gmail.com with the subject “InstantSaver privacy request”. Specify your request and the relevant social platform and username or user ID so we can locate your records. We may ask for information needed to verify that the records belong to you. Do not send your password or login cookies.

Requests can cover synced social profile information, associated notification tokens and support records we hold. Some information may need to be retained for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention or resolving disputes. Deleting app data does not delete your Instagram or Facebook account, remove data held independently by those platforms, or cancel an app-store subscription. Requests concerning data held independently by another provider should also be directed to that provider.

5. Retention and security

We retain personal information for the purposes described above, including providing the service, responding to support requests and meeting applicable legal or security obligations. Retention varies by record type and by the service provider processing it. Local files remain until removed by you, the app or the operating system. Contact us about retention or deletion of a specific record.

We use HTTPS connections for app services and rely on the access controls of our service providers. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

6. Website and third-party links

The InstantSaver landing page does not include analytics or advertising scripts. Website hosting providers may process technical request information such as IP addresses, browser details and access times to deliver and secure the site. Following a Google Play link or another external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

7. Children’s privacy

InstantSaver is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Social platforms also have their own age requirements.

8. Changes and contact

We will post updates on this page and revise the date above. Where required, material changes will also be communicated through an appropriate notice.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Himanshu Choudhary at bohimanshu@gmail.com.
